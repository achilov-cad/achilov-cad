# 3-Axis Micro Servo Gimbal for FPV Payload Integration

A compact, 3-axis articulated gimbal mechanism engineered for lightweight FPV camera stabilization and manual target tracking on small UAVs or ground robotic platforms.

---

## 📐 Engineering & Kinematic Features

* **3-DOF Articulation:** Complete 3-axis motion coverage (Yaw, Pitch, Roll) utilizing lightweight micro servos for fast response.
* **Compact Spatial Layout:** Optimized form-factor designed to fit restricted payloads and front-nose UAV mounts without blocking airflow.
* **Manufacturing Ready (DFM):** Structural brackets engineered for lightweight 3D printing (FDM/SLS) or CNC-milled sheet materials.
* **COTS Hardware Integration:** Designed around standard micro servo geometries, bearings, and FPV camera mounting interfaces.

---

## 📂 Deliverables & Repository Structure

* `3-Axis_Gimbal_Assembly.step` — Universal CAD STEP model with full geometry and mounting alignment.
* `Renders/` — Visual presentation renders and structural assembly views.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications

* **Application:** FPV / UAV Camera Stabilization & Tracking
* **Actuation:** 3x Micro Servos
* **Design Stack:** Fusion 360 (Parametric Assembly Design & COTS Integration)
