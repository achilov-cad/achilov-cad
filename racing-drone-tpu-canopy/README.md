# Aerodynamic TPU Canopy for FPV Racing Drone

An ultra-lightweight, high-durability FPV camera canopy designed for FPV racing micro-quadcopters. Features an optimized aerodynamic profile and integrated flip-over turtle fin.

---

## 📐 Engineering & DFM Features

* **Organic Surface Modeling:** Smooth curvilinear geometry engineered in Fusion 360 to minimize drag and resist crash impacts.
* **Integrated Turtle Fin:** Top-mounted aerodynamic fin designed to facilitate self-righting (Turtle Mode) after collisions.
* **Camera Angle & Protection:** Recessed lens cavity protecting the FPV micro camera while maintaining an optimal fixed tilt angle for high-speed racing.
* **Additive Manufacturing (DFM):** Engineered specifically for FDM 3D printing using Flexible TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) without requiring complex internal supports.

---
