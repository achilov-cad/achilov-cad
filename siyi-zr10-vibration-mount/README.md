# SIYI ZR10 Gimbal Camera — Vibration Isolation Mount

A custom-designed mechanical vibration isolation assembly engineered specifically to mount and decouple the **SIYI ZR10 2K 30x Hybrid Zoom Gimbal Camera** from multirotor drone frames and robotic payloads.

## 📐 Overview
Unstabilized high-frequency vibrations from drone motors severely degrade optical zoom performance and video quality. This assembly uses an elastomeric spherical damping configuration sandwiched between custom structural plates to decouple the SIYI ZR10 payload from airframe resonance.

* **Target Hardware:** SIYI ZR10 Gimbal Camera (Accurate CAD Integration)
* **CAD Software:** Fusion 360 (Parametric Assembly Design)
* **Manufacturing Processes:** CNC machining / Laser cutting (aluminum/carbon fiber plates) & 3D printing / SLA (bracket components)

---

## ⚙️ Key Engineering Features
1. **Accurate COTS Integration:** Modeled the SIYI ZR10 body and original mounting interface to guarantee precise center-of-gravity placement and clearance for 360° rotation.
2. **Dual-Plate Vibration Dampening:** Elastomeric dampening balls absorb dynamic pitch/roll vibrations during forward flight and high-velocity maneuvers.
3. **Parametric Mounting Interface:** Designed in Fusion 360 to easily adapt the SIYI ZR10 onto various drone airframes or robotic platforms without modifying the camera itself.
